"Think Success Think MM(DU)."

A private deemed-to-be university in Haryana, built up from a single engineering college in the 1990s into a multi-faculty campus. This page is one student's look at where the campus has been — and the field its newest engineers are training in: artificial intelligence.

History & standing

From an engineering college to a deemed university

MM(DU) began in 1995 as an engineering college affiliated with Kurukshetra University. In June 2007, the Ministry of Human Resource Development granted it deemed-to-be-university status under the UGC Act. It has since grown into a multi-campus institution offering close to 150 undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across engineering, medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, nursing, law, management, agriculture and biotechnology.

The field

Learning to build systems that learn

Artificial intelligence is the study of building systems that do things — recognising images, parsing language, predicting outcomes — that used to require a person's judgement. Machine learning, the part most computer-science students spend their degree on, is about getting those systems to improve from data rather than from hand-written rules. A typical B.Tech in CSE with an AI & ML specialisation moves from linear algebra and probability in year one toward neural networks, natural language processing and computer vision by the last.

Builds towardNeural nets, NLP, vision
Recognition

Rated among India's top institutions

NAAC, India's national accreditation body, has rated MM(DU) at its highest grade, A++ — placing it among the top 3% of institutions it has assessed. In NIRF's 2023 rankings, released by India's Ministry of Education, MM(DU) placed 78th among all universities in the country, with its pharmacy and medical institutes ranked separately among the top colleges in their own fields.
